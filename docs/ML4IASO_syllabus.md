## Course Overview

**Lecture 1 / Introduction.** A review of the probability and statistics background used throughout the course.

**Lectures 2–8 / Supervised Learning.** Learning to predict an output from labeled examples. We start with the two most basic strategies for regression and classification, then progressively build up ways to control model complexity and fight overfitting—first through ensembles of trees, then through regularization and kernel methods—before formalizing these ideas with a rigorous treatment of the bias-variance tradeoff and model selection. The block closes with support vector machines as a further case study.

**Lectures 9–12 / Unsupervised Learning.** Learning structure from data without labels: clustering, dimensionality reduction, probabilistic graphical models, and latent-variable models such as mixture models, the EM algorithm, and hidden Markov models.
